## Experiments

### Run against devnet, 2026-10-05 — no phone, no wallet

These settle the mechanism's core claims independently of Mobile Wallet
Adapter, because they test what the **runtime** does. That is the part of the
thesis Quittance does not implement and therefore cannot get wrong.

**5 of 5 passed.** Raw output in `evidence/experiments.json`, regenerated by
`node scripts/experiment-nonce.mjs`.

Two findings worth stating precisely, because an earlier version of this
experiment got both wrong and the corrections are the interesting part:

- **E6a.** Rebroadcasting identical bytes is not refused by the cluster — it
  is *deduplicated by signature* and answered from the already-confirmed
  transaction. Ten resends were all "accepted" and the effect still happened
  exactly once. The measurement that means anything is the destination
  balance, not the RPC response.
- **E6b.** The nonce protection proper. A **different** transaction built
  against the consumed nonce has a different signature, so deduplication
  cannot apply and the runtime must validate it — and it refuses with
  `Blockhash not found`. That is citation C2 demonstrated on a live cluster,
  and it is where invariant I1 actually lives.
