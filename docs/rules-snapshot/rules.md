### 2. SKR staking integrations are explicitly excluded

From the FAQ, verbatim:

> **WHAT IS SKR?**
> (SKR Staking Integrations do not qualify) SKR is the native asset of the
> Solana Mobile Ecosystem. The best SKR integration receives a $10,000 SKR
> prize.

The nonce lease as specified — the circle organizer **stakes SKR** to open a
lease — is a staking integration and does not qualify for the SKR prize as
designed. Recorded here against the specific claim it affects; see
`LIMITATIONS.md`.

### 3. No restriction on AI-assisted code

The full rules text was searched for `AI`, `artificial intelligence`, `LLM`,
`AI-generated`, and `AI-assisted`. **No match anywhere in 15,610 characters.**

The FAQ addresses it directly, and permissively:

> **CAN I VIBE-CODE MY APP?**
> Yes. How you build is up to you, as long as the work is your own and meets
> the submission requirements.

The brief is explicit too: *"Open to solo builders, agent orchestrators, vibe
conductors, and fully-fledged teams."*

The concern carried over from the first hackathon's rules does not apply to
CLOCK IN. The obligation that remains is that the work is the team's own, and
that commit history survives review: *"All winners are subject to technical
review, including code verification and follow-up questions."*

### 4. No required network

Nothing specifies devnet or mainnet. The requirement is that the app
*"interact meaningfully with the Solana network."* Devnet is not excluded.

### 5. The four criteria are equally weighted at 25% each

> Everything is scored across four equal 25% criteria.

## What to build

- Android only. The app must produce a functional APK.
- Must integrate the Solana Mobile Stack and Mobile Wallet Adapter.
- Designed for mobile from the ground up. Direct ports or PWA wrappers score
  poorly.
- Must interact meaningfully with the Solana network.

## What to submit

- A functional Android APK.
- A GitHub repository with the source code.
- A demo video showcasing functionality. The brief sets this at three minutes.
- A pitch deck or brief presentation.

> A working build, a repository someone else can clone and run, and a demo of
> three minutes. It has to run on a device rather than only in a simulator: a
> video nobody can install is not a submission.

## Evaluation criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Question asked |
|---|---|---|
| Stickiness & PMF | 25% | Does it resonate with the Seeker community, create habits, drive daily engagement? |
| User experience | 25% | Is the app intuitive, polished, and enjoyable to use? |
| Innovation / X-factor | 25% | How novel and creative is the idea? Does it stand out? |
| Presentation & demo quality | 25% | Was the idea communicated clearly? Does the demo showcase the core concept? |

Evaluation process, verbatim:

- Completion based on the demo video.
- Technical depth based on GitHub commits.
- Mobile-optimized user experience and usage of mobile features.
- Usage and interaction with the Solana network.
- Clarity and vision from the presentation.

## Eligibility

- Project must have been started within 3 months of the hackathon launch date.
- Only teams without VC or angel funding are eligible for USDC prizes.
- Pre-existing projects are allowed if they show significant new mobile
  development during the hackathon.
- Existing web apps may participate but must build an Android app with
  significant mobile-specific development.
- Direct ports, minimal conversions and PWA wrappers score poorly.
- Participants must be 18 or the local age of majority, legally able to
  contract, resident in an Eligible Country, subject to sanctions and
  compliance screening.

## Submission limits

> Only one submission per contestant, either as a solo participant or as part
> of a team. No multiple submissions will be allowed and will automatically
> result in a forfeit.

A submission may be edited while in draft, but not after the final submission
agreement is completed. Team composition is fixed at submission.

| 1st | $30,000 USDC + a Seeker for each team member |
| 2nd | $25,000 USDC |
| 3rd | $20,000 USDC |
| 4th | $15,000 USDC |
| 5th | $10,000 USDC |

$135,000 USDC total. There is a separate optional ORE matched prize: **one**
award across the entire top 10, matching that project's placement prize, paid
against milestones, requiring a live user-facing ORE integration.

## Tracks

> **ARE THERE TRACKS?**
> The main competition is judged as a single category. There is also a bonus
> SKR Integration prize for the best SKR integration.

## Publishing

Publishing to the Solana dApp Store is **not** required by the submission
deadline. Winners must publish within 30 calendar days of the announcement to
claim a cash prize.

## Testing without a Seeker

> **HOW DO I TEST WITHOUT A SEEKER?**
> You can build and test your APK on an Android emulator or any Android
> device.

## Repository access

> You do not need to invite judges separately. Our private GitHub connection
> is live where you install our Radiants Align GitHub app to your repo, choose
> the repos you want to share and give access.

A public repository is therefore not strictly required, though the submission
requirement is a repository someone else can clone and run.

## Disqualification

> Any team found to have provided false or misleading information on its
> registration or submission materials, or to have breached any rule of this
> hackathon, will be disqualified and forfeit all prizes, awards, and
> recognition. The organisers may make this determination at their sole
> discretion at any time; before, during, or after the event. Radiants may
> reclaim any prize already awarded.

## Legal

Organized by Radiants DAO Ltd. Governed by the laws of the British Virgin
Islands; disputes resolved by arbitration before the BVI International
Arbitration Centre. Entrants retain ownership of their project and grant the
organizer a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence for administration, judging,
archival and publicity. Submissions are not treated as confidential. Winner
announcements are provisional until KYC/AML checks via Sumsub complete for
every team member.
